ChronoKinesis Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-19

App: ChronoKinesis (Android package org.chronokinesis.game)

Developer: PTLDGames — contact: ptldgames@gmail.com

ChronoKinesis is an arcade game that can be played entirely offline. It sells no data and contains no advertising or in-app purchases. Optional online features are limited to Google Play Games, opt-in Firebase analytics and crash reporting, a local gift reminder, and a feedback form opened in your browser.

1. Optional Google Play Games sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides the app your Play Games player ID so it can unlock achievements, submit scores to leaderboards, and store game progress in your Google account for restoration on your devices. Scores submitted to a leaderboard are visible to other players through Google's leaderboard service.

The app stores no separate server copy. You can play without signing in. Play Games data is managed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can remove the app's Play Games data through your Google account's Play Games settings.

2. Optional gameplay analytics and crash reporting

After the title screen, the app asks whether you want to SHARE PLAY DATA. Nothing is collected until you explicitly choose to share, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings.

If you opt in, the app uses Google Firebase to collect:

  • Gameplay events: fixed-schema events about tutorial progress, runs, waves and bosses, weapon usage, in-game shop interactions using in-game currency, avatar selection, continues, leaderboards, and cloud saves. These events contain game identifiers and numbers, not free-form text, contact details, location, or identity fields.
  • Standard Analytics data: a random app-specific instance identifier, coarse device/app information, and session events such as first app open.
  • Crash and error reports: stack traces and device state tied to a random installation identifier, used only to find and fix bugs.

If you opt out or never answer, Firebase collection stays disabled, buffered events are discarded, and no analytics or crash report is sent. Choosing CONTINUE WITHOUT SHARING may cause the question to return once after several more plays; declining again makes that choice permanent. Revoking consent later disables both services, resets local Analytics data, and deletes unsent crash reports.

Firebase advertising consent signals remain denied. The app does not use Analytics for advertising and does not include an advertising SDK or request the Android Advertising ID.

3. In-game shop

The game has no real-money purchases. All shop items use fragments or boss cores earned in play. No payment method, billing address, Play purchase record, or purchase history is collected by the app.

4. Optional gift reminders

The game can post one local reminder when the recurring free gift is ready. It is opt-in: Android asks for notification permission only after you have claimed a gift, and you can decline. The reminder is scheduled and shown on your device by Android's alarm and notification services. No reminder data is sent anywhere, no server is involved, and your choice is stored only in the local save. Turn reminders on or off under Settings → GAME → FREE GIFT REMINDER or in Android's notification settings.

5. Optional feedback form

Settings → ONLINE → SEND FEEDBACK opens a Google Form in your browser. The game attaches nothing and sends nothing on its own. Only what you type is submitted, and only when you press the form's Send button. The form asks for a description, where it happened, the game version and phone model, and an optional email address if you want a reply. Responses are stored by Google Forms in the developer's Google account, used to fix problems and improve the game, and deleted once handled. Google's privacy policy applies to the form.

Data retention

Firebase Analytics event data is retained for 14 months and then deleted automatically. Crash reports are retained for 90 days under Crashlytics' standard policy. Play Games data remains in your Google account until you delete it. Feedback responses are deleted after they are handled.

Data sharing and selling

No data is sold. Optional data is processed only by the service needed for the feature you choose: Firebase, Play Games, or Google Forms. It is not used for advertising and is not shared with other third parties.

Children

ChronoKinesis is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information beyond what is described above.

Your choices and deletion

  • Play without Google sign-in: no Play Games data is provided to the app.
  • Decline or revoke SHARE PLAY DATA: Analytics/Crashlytics collection stops, local Analytics data is reset, and unsent crash reports are deleted.
  • Delete cloud saves using the in-game cloud controls or remove the app's data through your Google account's Play Games settings.
  • Disable gift reminders in the game or Android notification settings.
  • For another deletion request, contact ptldgames@gmail.com with no personal details beyond what is needed to act on the request.

ChronoKinesis Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-24 App: ChronoKinesis (Android package org.chronokinesis.game) Developer: PTLDGames — contact: ptldgames@gmail.com

ChronoKinesis is an offline arcade game. It shows no ads, contains no ad SDKs, never collects your device's Advertising ID, and sells no data. The app collects data in exactly two situations, both optional and described below.

1. Optional Google Play Games sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides the app your Play Games player ID so it can:

  • unlock achievements,
  • submit your scores to leaderboards (scores you submit are visible to other players through Google's leaderboard service), and
  • store your game progress (Saved Games) in your Google account so it can be restored on your devices.

The app stores no copy of this data outside your device and your Google account. You can play without signing in; nothing above is collected then. Play Games data is managed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; you can remove the app's Play Games data through your Google account's Play Games settings.

2. Optional gameplay analytics and crash reporting (explicit opt-in)

After the title screen the app asks whether you want to SHARE PLAY DATA. Nothing is collected until you explicitly choose to share, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings.

If you opt in, the app uses Google Firebase to collect:

  • Gameplay events — anonymous, fixed-schema events about how a play session went: tutorial progress, run start/end (score, kills, how the run ended), wave and boss outcomes, weapon usage summaries, in-game shop (Forge) interactions with in-game currency amounts, mech selection, continue decisions, leaderboard actions, and cloud-save actions. Every event carries only game identifiers and numbers (app version, game mode, ruleset id, run counter). No free-form text, no contact data, no location data, and no identity fields exist in the schema.
  • Standard Firebase Analytics data — an app-instance identifier (random, app-specific, not the Advertising ID), coarse device and app information (device model, OS version, app version), and session events such as first app open.
  • Crash and error reports (Firebase Crashlytics) — stack traces and device state at the time of a crash, tied to a random installation identifier, used solely to find and fix bugs.

If you opt out or never answer, Firebase collection stays disabled: analytics storage is denied, no events are sent, and events buffered before your choice are discarded.

If you revoke consent later, the app immediately disables Analytics and Crashlytics, resets the locally stored analytics data (including the app-instance identifier), and deletes any unsent crash reports.

Advertising: the app requests no advertising-ID permission, keeps Advertising ID collection disabled, and denies every advertising consent signal (ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization) even when you opt in to sharing play data.

Data retention

Analytics event data is retained by Google Firebase for 14 months and then deleted automatically. Crash reports are retained for 90 days by Crashlytics' standard policy.

Data sharing and selling

Collected data is processed by Google (Firebase, Play Games) as a service provider on the developer's behalf. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with any other third party.

Children

ChronoKinesis is not directed at children under 13, and the app collects no personal information beyond what is described above.

Your choices and deletion

  • Play without Google sign-in: no Play Games data exists.
  • Decline or revoke SHARE PLAY DATA in Settings: analytics/crash collection stops, local analytics data is reset, unsent crash reports are deleted.
  • Delete cloud saves: use the in-game cloud controls, or remove the app's data in your Google account's Play Games settings.
  • For any other deletion request, contact ptldgames@gmail.com with no personal details beyond what's needed to act on the request.
